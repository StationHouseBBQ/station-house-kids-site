Our story
These books come from our real life: the shift nights, the toy drives, the dinners at the station. I wrote them so my girls would know what to do on the nights I'm gone: pray for Daddy and his crew.

Anthony Fonseca, firefighter, husband to Allison, dad to Arabella and Alaina

Written by a firefighter. Anthony has spent 17 years in the fire service. The shift nights, the saved plates and the paper-chain countdowns come straight from his family's life.

Built around prayer. Every night page has a Bible verse (NIV), a question to talk about and a short prayer a child can say on their own.

Made for every first-responder family. Firefighters, police, nurses, the military: if someone you love works the night shift, these books are for your kids.
